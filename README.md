# Shane's Tattoo Shop — Master Ink Studio & Business Enterprise

An ultra-premium, fully-developed tattoo shop web application built for **Shane's Tattoo Shop**. Featuring a public landing experience, customer consultation tools, an Owner Command Portal for Shane (pay stubs coordinator, IRS yearly tax manager, and no-limits web page builder CMS), and a dedicated Artist Workstation.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Architecture

### 1. 🎨 Customer Experience & Public Landing Page
- **Hero Sanctuary**: High-energy tattoo aesthetic, live walk-in availability ticker, hospital-grade autoclave accreditation, and direct consultation CTAs.
- **Interactive Body Map, Pain & Cost Estimator**:
  - Interactive anatomical human model with Front & Back view toggle.
  - Sensation and pain rating gauges (1–10 scale) for Sternum/Ribs, Outer Forearm, Inner Bicep, Collarbone, Spine, Thigh, Neck, and Hands.
  - Live project price calculator based on dimension scaling (2" Flash to Full Backpiece), style complexity, and artist tier.
- **Exclusive Flash Art Gallery**:
  - Filterable by Japanese Irezumi, Fine Line Botanical, Neo-Traditional, Sacred Geometry, and Dark Realism.
  - Fullscreen lightbox zoom, estimated needle hours, and instant flash claim with deposit hold.
- **AI Tattoo Concept Studio**:
  - Enter custom prompts (e.g., *"Cyberpunk samurai mask with neon cherry blossoms"*), explore style blueprints, and submit concepts directly to Shane or resident artists for booking.
- **Resident Artist Roster**:
  - Shane Vance (Owner & Master Artist), Elena "Viper" Ramos, Jax "Kross" Mercer, Maya Lin. Bios, hourly rates, specialties, awards, and portfolios.
- **Before / After Cover-Up Slider**:
  - Interactive split-image drag comparison showing old tattoo transformations.
- **Clinical Aftercare & Healing Day-by-Day Tracker**:
  - 4-phase healing protocol (Saniderm -> Gentle Wash -> Peeling -> UV Shield) with interactive daily checklist and printable aftercare cheat sheets.
- **Live Walk-in Board & SMS Waitlist Kiosk**:
  - Real-time queue tracker with live wait minutes and SMS waitlist registration.
- **Studio Pro Shop & Merch**:
  - Organic tattoo balms, heavyweight French terry hoodies, foaming cleansers, and gift cards with slide-out shopping bag and simulated checkout.
- **Audio Machine Sound Engine**:
  - Web Audio API rotary tattoo machine hum generator and interactive sound effects.
- **6-Step Live Booking Wizard**:
  - Service selection -> Artist picker -> Placement & dimensions -> Date & time calendar -> Medical screening -> Deposit payment with confetti confirmation.

### 2. 👑 Owner Portal — "Shane's Command Center"
Accessible via the **Owner Portal** button (or 1-click test login for Shane Vance):
- **Executive Financial Dashboard**:
  - Real-time KPIs: $483,100 Gross Revenue YTD, $99,650 Net Taxable Income, $238,400 in 1099 disbursements.
  - Interactive monthly revenue vs expense bar charts and artist performance breakdown.
- **Pay Stub Coordinator & Payroll Engine (Core Requirement)**:
  - Coordinate weekly and bi-weekly payouts for Elena, Jax, Maya, Shane, and guest artists.
  - Commission splits (60/40, 65/35, 70/30, 100% Owner Draw), client tip pass-throughs, supply deductions (needles, ink cartridges, PPE), and credit card processing fee shares.
  - **Pay Stub Generator**: Create new pay stubs with custom date ranges, auto-computed net payouts, and payment channels.
  - **Official Pay Stub PDF Engine**: View and print officially formatted earnings statements with shop EIN, contractor details, and itemized deduction breakdowns.
- **Yearly Revenue & Tax Coordinator for IRS (Core Requirement)**:
  - Multi-year tax selector (2026 YTD, 2025, 2024).
  - **Schedule C (Form 1040) Deductions Breakdown**: COGS (inks/needles), Line 11 Contract Labor, Line 20 Studio Lease & Utilities, Line 13 Machine Depreciation, Line 8 Advertising/Web, Line 16 Biohazard licensing and liability insurance.
  - **Quarterly Estimated Taxes (Form 1040-ES)** tracker with safe harbor projection checks and payment confirmation codes.
  - **1099-NEC Contractor Summary Table** with W-9 status verification and 1-click 1099-NEC form generation.
  - **One-Click Tax Packet Export**: Print and download formatted annual tax packets and CSV exports.
- **No-Limits CMS Web Page Builder (Core Requirement)**:
  - Create unlimited custom landing pages and event promotions on the web (e.g., `/events/guest-spot-2026`, `/piercings-body-mod`, `/promos/halloween-flash-night`).
  - Modular content blocks: Hero Banners, Rich Text & Story blocks, Pricing & Flash Tier menus, Image galleries, and countdowns.
  - Live desktop & mobile device previews.
  - Published pages automatically register routes and appear in the public site navigation!
- **Studio Appointments Pipeline & Inventory Tracking**:
  - Filter bookings, update deposit statuses, track Kwadron needle cartridges and Dynamic Black ink stock with quick supplier reordering.
